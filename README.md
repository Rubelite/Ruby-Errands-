# Ruby-Errands-
Ruby Errands is an Abuja-based errand and delivery service designed to give busy individuals, professionals, parents, entrepreneurs, and small businesses back their time.  The platform makes it easier for customers to request everyday errands such as market runs, grocery shopping, item pickups, shopping assistance, and local deliveries. 
Ruby Errands is a modern web platform for an Abuja-based errand and delivery service. The application allows customers to discover services, submit errand requests, manage their orders, and communicate with Ruby Errands for fulfillment.

The platform is designed around a simple goal: give people back their time.

Overview

Ruby Errands helps individuals, professionals, parents, entrepreneurs, and small businesses outsource everyday tasks such as:

Market and grocery runs
Shopping
Item pickups
Local deliveries
Everyday errands

The application provides a digital interface for requesting services while supporting the operational workflow required to manage and complete customer orders.

Errand Management

The platform is designed to support the complete errand lifecycle:

Customer selects a service.
Customer provides the required task details.
Customer submits the request.
Ruby Errands reviews and confirms the order.
The errand is assigned and processed.
The customer receives updates.
The order is completed.
